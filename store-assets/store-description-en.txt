Highlight, take notes and translate right on the page you're reading. Select text to see its meaning; words and passages you save light up whenever you meet them again, and notes you pin to a page are right there the next time you open it. Made for anyone reading the web in a language that isn't their own.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

✦ SELECT TO TRANSLATE, WITHOUT LEAVING THE PAGE
• Select a word or a whole passage → a small bar appears: Translate · Save · Listen · Copy
• Translate in a floating card into your own language, even long passages (up to 5,000 characters)
• Need another language? Click the language name at the top of the card — English, Japanese, Korean, Chinese, Spanish, French, German, Portuguese, Arabic, Hindi… — it's remembered next time
• English words get IPA pronunciation and audio; dictionary links for English (Cambridge), Japanese (Jisho), Chinese, Korean (Naver)
• Shortcuts: Alt+Shift+D to translate, Alt+Shift+H to save (change them at chrome://extensions/shortcuts)

✦ SAVE ONCE, SEE IT HIGHLIGHTED EVERYWHERE
• Click Save → the translation is already filled in, just press Enter
• Saved words are highlighted on every website; hover to see your meaning, pronunciation and notes
• Save whole passages: reopen the page and the passage is highlighted in place, with a link back to the source
• Changed your mind? There's always Undo

✦ NOTES ON THE PAGE ITSELF
• Right-click an empty spot → "Pin a note on this page": a small sticky note that lives on that page
• Drag it anywhere, collapse it, hide it without losing anything
• Every saved word or passage has its own note field for your own explanation

✦ NOT JUST FOR VOCABULARY
• 5 types, 5 colors: Vocabulary · Important · To-do · Question · Reference
• Tag items by course or project
• Great for students reading textbooks and professionals reading technical docs

✦ EASY TO ADJUST
• Hover the floating bar and click ⋯ to turn it off, turn Highlight Note off on the current site, or change the translation language — right where you are
• Settings have a search box, show which settings you've changed, and reset per section
• Interface in English or Vietnamese

✦ TIDY AND EASY TO FIND AGAIN
• Popup with quick search, filters by type, tag, or what's on the current page
• Side Panel that stays next to the page while you read (Alt+Shift+S)
• Add many words at once: paste straight from Excel or Google Sheets
• Export to Markdown for Obsidian or Notion; back up to a JSON file

✦ SPACED REVIEW (WHEN YOU WANT IT)
• Flashcards on a 1 → 3 → 7 → 14… day schedule, review with Space · 1 · 2 · 3
• Off by default — no learning, no nagging

✦ YOUR DATA, ON YOUR COMPUTER
• No account, no ads, no tracking
• Only the text you choose to translate is sent to Google Translate / dictionaries
• Sync between computers through one file in your own Google Drive — only when you click, and it asks before changing or deleting anything
• Nothing is downloaded automatically; weekly backups only if you turn them on

✦ WHY "READ DATA ON ALL WEBSITES"?
To highlight your saved words on any page you read. The extension doesn't collect or send your browsing history anywhere. You can turn it off on any site (email, banking…): open the popup → Turn off here, or click ⋯ on the floating bar.
